# Canonical contract

Live Operations prioritizes what is blocked, incomplete, degraded, uncertain, or fairness-relevant rather than foregrounding leaderboard information.

Organizer views distinguish Judge readiness, Panel composition, Encounter state, Scorecard not-started/Draft/Finalized/amendment/uncertain states, recusal, paper fallback, and recovery needs.

Permanent Panel reassignment remains distinct from one-off Encounter substitution. Current membership changes never rewrite prior Encounter participants.

Organizer may coordinate, prompt, reassign, excuse, capture paper, recover, or invoke governed invalidation/correction, but operational urgency never turns Organizer into Judge author.

Completing the live event ends ordinary live judging and Judge private-evaluation Access while carrying permitted unresolved work into reconciliation.
