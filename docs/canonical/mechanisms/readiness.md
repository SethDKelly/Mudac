# Canonical contract

Readiness is derived from authoritative source state and policy; it is not an editable checklist flag.

MUDAC uses distinct readiness projections, including Competition readiness, Judge readiness, Ranking readiness, and Finalization readiness. Each answers whether a specific next operation may legitimately proceed.

Blocking conditions differ from warnings. A warning may persist while a subject is Ready when policy permits proceeding.

Acknowledge does not resolve source state. If source conditions change, readiness recomputes.

See [Organizer Preparation](../experience/organizer-preparation.md) and [Reconciliation & Finalization](../experience/reconciliation-finalization.md).
