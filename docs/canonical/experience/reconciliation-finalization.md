# Canonical contract

After Event Completed, Organizer work proceeds from authoritative evidence to Coverage/eligibility, Ranking readiness, declared tie requirements, Awards, Finalization readiness, and explicit Competition Finalization.

Reconciliation issues are projections of source state, not manually closable tickets. A numerical result may be visible while Coverage or Ranking readiness is incomplete.

Rank is inspectable/explainable but never directly edited. Rank-derived and discretionary Award decisions remain visibly distinct.

Finalization is high consequence and must revalidate authoritative state at commit time. Success establishes an Official Outcome Revision; publication remains separate.

Post-Finalization corrected calculations are shown alongside the still-current official revision until a successor is explicitly confirmed.
