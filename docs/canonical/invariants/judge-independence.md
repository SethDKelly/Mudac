# INV-001 — Judge Independence

During ordinary judging, a Judge may access their own evaluation work but not peer Scorecards/Notes, Panel mean, Team Aggregate, Coverage, Rank, or standings.

Finalizing one's own Scorecard does not unlock those comparison signals during the judging period.

This protects independent judgment and applies across interactive, print, accessible, and degraded representations.

See [Scorecard](../concepts/scorecard.md) and [Anonymity & Disclosure](../policies/anonymity-disclosure.md).
