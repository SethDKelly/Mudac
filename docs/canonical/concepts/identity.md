# Purpose

Maintain continuity that actions and Participation episodes belong to the same human identity.

# Canonical contract

Identity answers who the human is. It may persist across Competition occurrences and may be established, reverified, or disabled according to later authentication architecture.

Identity alone does not grant current Competition authority, restore prior Panel assignment, or imply permanent Judge/Organizer role.

# Boundaries

Authentication mechanism is an implementation choice. Current event authority is represented by [Participation](participation.md) and constrained by [Access](access.md).

See [Judge Onboarding](../experience/judge-onboarding.md).
