# Canonical contract

A Criterion is a stable subordinate evaluation dimension within a [Rubric](../concepts/rubric.md), with order, title, guidance, valid score domain, contribution semantics, and note policy.

Criterion Notes and overall Notes are qualitative evidence within a [Scorecard](../concepts/scorecard.md). They do not independently alter numeric results unless explicit future policy says otherwise.

Missing, zero, and not-applicable meanings remain distinct. Required Criterion responses/Notes may block Scorecard Finalization without turning missing values into numeric values.
