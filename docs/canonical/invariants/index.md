# Cross-Cutting Invariants

Stable normative constraints that span multiple Concepts, policies, mechanisms, or experience contracts. Their `INV-*` identifiers are durable cross-reference interfaces governed by [Stable Rule Identifiers & Cross-Reference Contract](../governance/rule-identifiers.md).

* [INV-001 — Judge Independence](judge-independence.md#inv-001) - Judge evaluation remains independent from peer results and standings.
* [INV-002 — One Logical Scorecard](one-logical-scorecard.md#inv-002) - One Judge Participation × Encounter yields at most one weighted evaluation.
* [INV-003 — Missing Is Never Zero](missing-never-zero.md#inv-003) - Absence of evaluation is not a numeric judgment.
* [INV-004 — Organizer Does Not Become Judge Author](organizer-not-judge-author.md#inv-004) - Process/capture authority cannot silently become evaluation authorship.
* [INV-005 — Current vs Historical Truth](current-vs-historical-truth.md#inv-005) - Current corrected state does not overwrite what was historically observed.
* [INV-006 — Calculated Is Not Official](calculated-not-official.md#inv-006) - Computability/readiness does not establish declared official outcome.
* [INV-007 — Official Is Not Automatically Public](official-not-automatically-public.md#inv-007) - Finalization and publication remain separate authority boundaries.
* [INV-008 — Capture-Channel Parity](capture-channel-parity.md#inv-008) - Paper/electronic paths preserve one evaluation model and weight.
* [INV-009 — Accessibility Semantic Parity](accessibility-semantic-parity.md#inv-009) - Accessibility adaptations preserve domain meaning and authority.
* [INV-010 — Truthful Authority Under Uncertainty](truthful-authority-under-uncertainty.md#inv-010) - Unknown persistence/authority cannot be presented as confirmed success.

The invariant file/anchor is the rule owner. This index routes; it does not duplicate the full rule bodies.
