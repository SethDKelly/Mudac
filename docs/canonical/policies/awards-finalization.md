# Canonical contract

Rank-derived Awards consume a ranking-ready declared result and are confirmed by Organizer without contradicting the rule. Discretionary Awards are authorized human decisions and must remain visibly non-mathematical unless their selection policy defines otherwise.

Finalization is an explicit high-consequence operation gated by reconciled evidence, Coverage/eligibility, Rubric compatibility, ranking readiness, required tie resolution, authoritative Evaluation Policy, and required/consistent Award decisions.

Successful Finalization creates an [Official Outcome Revision](../mechanisms/official-outcome-revision.md). It does not publish results.

Post-Finalization corrections produce successor official revisions only after explicit reconciliation/confirmation.
