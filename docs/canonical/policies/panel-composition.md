# Canonical contract

Competition policy may define desired Panel composition capacities such as Academic, Business, and Technical.

A Judge's expertise is Participation metadata; the capacity they satisfy is Panel-specific. Baseline behavior assigns at most one required capacity per Judge per Panel while allowing at-large members where policy permits.

Composition status may be Compliant, Degraded, or Noncompliant. Explicit exceptions preserve the actual shortfall rather than pretending composition requirements were met. Such exceptions are scoped governed exceptions under [Operational Exception & Override Governance](operational-exception-governance.md#opg-001); they do not rewrite actual Panel or [Evaluation Occurrence](../concepts/evaluation-occurrence.md) participation and do not waive unrelated evaluation/authorship constraints.

For a Panel-backed occurrence, current Panel membership is only an intended grouping input. [Phase 011 composition](../synchronizations/evaluation-occurrence-obligation.md) confirms the actual starting evaluator set at occurrence begin. A nominal member who is absent or recused before begin does not become a fictitious occurrence participant or Evaluation Obligation merely to make composition appear compliant.

When actual starting evaluators are Degraded or Noncompliant, the occurrence may begin only where policy permits proceeding and any required governed exception records the preserved shortfall and authorized consequence.

Final day-of-event staffing is operational readiness rather than a universal Competition Ready prerequisite.

See [Panel Membership & Composition](../mechanisms/panel-membership-composition.md).
