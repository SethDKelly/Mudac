# Purpose

Define current MUDAC composition from eligible authoritative evaluation evidence through factual Coverage, calculated Aggregate/Rank, Award authority, lifecycle Finalization and explicit official Outcome Declaration without collapsing calculated, recognized, official or public meanings.

This contract is the current Phase 011-G owner for legacy synchronizations 11, 13 and 14, the derived-refresh portion of legacy 09, and the derived/Award/declaration consequences of legacy 12.

Export/Publication consequences are owned by [External Representation, Currency & Publication Release Composition](external-representation-publication-release.md).

# Authority boundary

- **Scorecard / Evaluation Occurrence / Evaluation Obligation / Rubric Version state** supplies current evidence-eligibility facts.
- **Coverage** derives factual sufficiency only.
- **Aggregate** derives numerical combination of current eligible authoritative Scorecards.
- **Rank** derives ordering over a supplied rank-eligible Team set under declared policy.
- **Award** owns recognition definition, conferral, revocation and correction history.
- **Competition** owns lifecycle Finalization.
- **Outcome Declaration** owns explicit official-result declaration/currentness/history.
- **Export/Publication** own representation/release later; official never implies public.

No mechanism above may impersonate another owner.

# Current eligible evaluation evidence

Current outcome derivation may consume a Scorecard only when the supplied current facts establish that:

- one current eligible authoritative Scorecard Version exists;
- its structural Evaluator/Subject/Occurrence/Basis binding is correct for the intended scope;
- its Evaluation Obligation is historically Satisfied by that logical Scorecard;
- the Evaluation Occurrence remains currently eligible for evaluative use;
- the bound Rubric/Evaluation Basis remains eligible for that use under current policy;
- no current evidence invalidation/correction state makes the Scorecard ineligible.

Historical satisfaction/authorship may remain true even when current outcome eligibility is false.

# Coverage composition

Coverage consumes current eligible evidence plus the applicable requirement basis.

Its factual result remains:

```text
Satisfied | Incomplete
```

Governed exception disposition is independent.

```text
Coverage = Incomplete
Exception = Accepted for a specific consequence
```

is valid composition.

An exception never fabricates evidence, converts missing to zero, mutates an obligation, or rewrites Coverage to Satisfied.

# Aggregate composition

Aggregate uses current eligible authoritative Scorecards under current Evaluation Policy.

Baseline weighting remains one equal unit per eligible individual Judge Scorecard. Missing evidence remains missing.

A numeric Aggregate may exist while Coverage is Incomplete.

Therefore Aggregate existence does not establish Coverage satisfaction, rank eligibility or official authority.

# Rank composition

Rank consumes:

- a supplied rank-eligible Team set;
- current Aggregate values/basis;
- Division scope;
- current Evaluation Policy including comparison precision/tie rules.

MUDAC derives rank eligibility before invoking Rank from current Coverage plus any explicit exception disposition and other applicable eligibility conditions.

Rank never changes Coverage facts and exposes no generic manual edit action.

# Derived currentness

Coverage/Aggregate/Rank are basis-relative derived results.

When a material input or governing policy basis changes:

1. prior derivation becomes non-current/affected for ordinary current use;
2. recomputation derives the new current result;
3. prior basis/result may remain reconstructible for historical explanation;
4. no source Concept is mutated by recomputation.

This is semantic currency, not a cache/job design.

# Rank-derived Award

Application action: **Confer Rank-Derived Award**.

Participant:

- `Award.confer` using current Rank as supplied SelectionBasis.

Conditions include:

- current Award definition and scope;
- `Derived` selection semantics;
- current applicable Rank basis;
- recipient consistency with Award rule;
- tie/cardinality rules satisfied;
- legitimate conferral authority.

Award owns the resulting recognition.

Later Rank recalculation never silently moves an existing Award. Correction/revocation/reconferral remains explicit Award behavior.

# Discretionary Award

Application action: **Confer Discretionary Award**.

The authorized actor deliberately chooses an eligible Recipient under an Award whose definition permits discretionary selection.

Rank visibility does not make the Award mathematically derived.

# Award affectedness

When source/Rank basis changes, application composition determines whether the existing Award remains consistent or requires explicit review/correction.

No source correction automatically revokes or transfers recognition.

# Closeout Basis

Before ordinary official closeout, MUDAC assembles a supplied **Closeout Basis**. This is composition data, not a Concept.

It identifies, as applicable:

- Competition in `Event Completed`;
- current Evaluation Policy;
- current Coverage plus attributable exception dispositions;
- current Aggregate/Rank basis for required outcomes;
- required tie resolution/policy result;
- required/current Award decisions;
- unresolved correction/reconciliation conditions relevant to closeout;
- exact accepted OutcomeBasis intended for declaration.

# Finalize Competition & Declare Outcome

Application action: **Finalize Competition & Declare Outcome**.

Participants:

- `Competition.finalize`;
- `OutcomeDeclaration.declare`.

Ordinary conditions:

- Competition is `Event Completed`;
- authorized closeout actor/context;
- supplied Closeout Basis satisfies Awards & Finalization Policy;
- blocking reconciliation/correction conditions are resolved;
- OutcomeBasis identifies the exact accepted evidence/policy/Coverage/Rank/Award basis strongly enough for reconstruction;
- no initial current declaration already exists for the same closeout scope.

Semantic success requires:

```text
Competition = Finalized
AND
current explicit Outcome Declaration exists
```

This is a conceptual success condition, not a prescribed transaction.

Competition still owns lifecycle; Outcome Declaration still owns declaration content/history.

# Calculated != official != public

Current Coverage/Aggregate/Rank and Award state may exist before official outcome declaration.

Only explicit Outcome Declaration establishes official result authority.

Official declaration does not generate Export or Publication and does not make results public.

# Post-Finalization correction

Competition remains Finalized after legitimate source correction.

Application composition then:

- re-derives affected Coverage/Aggregate/Rank as needed;
- reviews Award consistency and uses explicit Award actions if correction is required;
- identifies the current Outcome Declaration as Affected when its immutable basis materially depends on the changed source.

No corrected calculation becomes official automatically.

# Identify Official Outcome Affected

Application reaction: **Identify Official Outcome Affected**.

Participant:

- `OutcomeDeclaration.identifyAffected`.

Conditions:

- a current declaration exists;
- an actual material dependency of its OutcomeBasis changed/became invalid/ineligible;
- attributable affected reason/basis exists.

Postconditions:

- declaration becomes `Affected`;
- declaration content/history remains unchanged;
- Competition remains Finalized;
- newer calculations are not official by implication.

Affected means latest declared authority known to require correction/review; it is not silent supersession.

# Confirm Successor Outcome Declaration

Application action: **Confirm Successor Outcome Declaration**.

Participant:

- `OutcomeDeclaration.confirmSuccessor`.

Conditions:

- current declaration is Affected;
- corrected evidence/derived/Award basis is reconciled;
- successor closeout policy conditions are satisfied;
- authorized declaring authority explicitly confirms the corrected OutcomeBasis.

Postconditions:

- successor becomes Current official authority;
- predecessor becomes Superseded historical authority;
- predecessor/correction path remains reconstructible;
- Competition remains Finalized;
- Export/Publication remain unchanged until their own actions occur.

# Composition invariants

1. Historical obligation satisfaction does not prove current evidence eligibility.
2. Coverage factual sufficiency is never rewritten by exception disposition.
3. Missing evidence is never zero.
4. Aggregate existence does not prove Coverage satisfaction or rank eligibility.
5. Rank consumes a supplied rank-eligible set and declared policy; it owns no eligibility facts.
6. Calculated Rank is not official authority.
7. Rank-derived Award selection basis does not transfer Award conferral ownership.
8. Recalculation never silently moves recognition.
9. Competition Finalization and Outcome Declaration remain independently owned.
10. Ordinary MUDAC closeout semantically succeeds only when both Finalization and explicit initial declaration are established.
11. Official outcome authority requires explicit Outcome Declaration.
12. Official does not imply public.
13. Post-Finalization source correction never rolls Competition backward.
14. Affected declaration remains latest declared official authority until explicit successor confirmation.
15. Corrected calculations/Awards do not silently create a successor declaration.

# Exceptional no-ordinary-result closeout

Phase 016-F validates a closeout case not explicit in the earlier ordinary-path wording: the Competition is ready to leave ordinary operation, but authoritative evidence/policy cannot legitimately produce an ordinary ranked result for a required scope.

MUDAC handles this without a new Result Concept and without fabricating Rank.

The applicable policy may establish an attributable **Exceptional Closeout Disposition** when:

- the affected Competition/result scope is explicit;
- current Coverage/evidence/eligibility facts are reconstructible;
- an ordinary ranked result cannot legitimately be produced;
- any remaining correction, replacement, successor-evaluation, tie, or reconciliation paths are resolved to the extent required by policy;
- the exceptional authorizer and reason are attributable;
- the resulting exceptional OutcomeBasis explicitly states the absence/unavailability of the ordinary result and any unaffected result/recognition scope.

The existing coordinated application action **Finalize Competition & Declare Outcome** may then consume either:

1. an ordinary reconciled Closeout Basis with the required Ranking Ready outcome state; or
2. a policy-authorized exceptional Closeout Basis containing the Exceptional Closeout Disposition and an explicit exceptional OutcomeBasis.

For the exceptional path:

```text
Coverage may remain Incomplete
Ranking Readiness may remain false
ordinary Rank may be unavailable
rank-derived Award may be unavailable

but

Exceptional Closeout Disposition = authorized
Finalization Readiness = true for exceptional closeout
→ Competition.finalize
+ OutcomeDeclaration.declare(exceptional OutcomeBasis)
```

Semantic success remains:

```text
Competition = Finalized
AND
current explicit Outcome Declaration exists
```

The declaration is an official statement of the exceptional outcome condition; it is not an invented winner, fabricated Rank, or hidden conversion of uncertainty into an ordinary result.

## Unknown versus exceptional official outcome

Preserve:

```text
unknown / unresolved result
  != policy-resolved exceptional no-result outcome
  != ordinary ranked result
```

Repeated or ambiguous action intent, stale calculation, unresolved evidence, or unknown closeout authority cannot be converted into an Exceptional Closeout Disposition merely to obtain closure.

## Additional composition invariants

16. An ordinary ranked result is not required when policy explicitly authorizes a reconstructible exceptional no-result OutcomeBasis.
17. Exceptional closeout never mutates factual Coverage, fabricates Rank, or confers rank-derived Award authority.
18. Unknown/unresolved authority cannot be presented as an official exceptional outcome.
19. Outcome Declaration remains the owner of official exceptional-outcome authority/history; no new Result/No-Result Concept is introduced.
