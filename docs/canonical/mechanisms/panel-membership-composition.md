# Canonical contract

Within MUDAC, Panel Membership relates a Judge [Participation](../concepts/participation.md) identity to a [Panel](../concepts/panel.md) for an effective interval and may record the composition capacity the Judge satisfies for that Panel.

Expertise is Participation metadata; composition capacity is Panel-specific use of that supplied information. One Judge normally satisfies at most one required capacity for a Panel unless governing policy says otherwise.

Current membership never rewrites historical [Evaluation Occurrence](../concepts/evaluation-occurrence.md) participant history.

Panel membership alone does not create [Evaluation Obligation](../concepts/evaluation-obligation.md) state.

Current application composition is owned by [Competitor Context, Evaluation Occurrence & Obligation Composition](../synchronizations/evaluation-occurrence-obligation.md): Panel membership may supply candidate starting evaluators, but actual starting participants are confirmed at occurrence begin and individual responsibilities are then established separately.

A Panel membership change after an occurrence begins does not silently alter that occurrence or its obligations. If one operational decision should update both the reusable Panel and a live occurrence, the application must coordinate those actions explicitly.

See [Panel Composition Policy](../policies/panel-composition.md).
