# Purpose

004-C performs the semantic cutover from phase-centric current-truth retrieval to concise canonical knowledge ownership for the product/domain and conceptual UX baseline.

The objective is not to rewrite Phases 001–003. It is to extract one preferred current owner for each independently retrievable meaning while preserving the phase corpus as rationale and source lineage.

# Extraction result

004-C creates and accepts:

* **15 Design Concept documents** — one for each accepted MUDAC Concept;
* **9 derived/supporting mechanism documents** — Team attributes, Criterion/Notes, Panel membership/composition, readiness, Coverage, Aggregate, Rank, Reconciliation, and Official Outcome Revision;
* **6 Design Policy documents** — Evaluation Policy, anonymity/disclosure, Panel composition, correction/authority, Awards/finalization, and continuity/paper;
* **10 cross-cutting invariant documents** — only constraints whose repeated cross-domain use justifies a stable independent owner;
* **9 Experience Contract documents** — the current Phase 003 user-facing interaction semantics.

No additional MUDAC Concept is introduced.

# Ownership discipline

Canonical documents are intentionally shorter than phase records.

A Concept document owns Concept identity, purpose, state/actions/operational principle, and Concept-specific boundaries. It links to policy, mechanisms, invariants, and experience instead of absorbing those concerns.

A mechanism document owns the meaning of a derived/supporting construct but does not promote it into the Concept catalog.

A policy document owns configurable/governing semantics. A mechanism consumes policy rather than duplicating policy configuration.

A standalone invariant exists only when a constraint is cross-cutting, repeatedly depended upon, and valuable as a stable future architecture/test reference.

An Experience Contract owns user-facing meaning, not component/framework choices.

# Canonical authority cutover

For subjects extracted and indexed by 004-C, the corresponding `docs/canonical/` document is now the preferred current owner.

Historical Phase 001–003 records remain authoritative evidence of design evolution and remain valid sources for rationale, but consumers should no longer reconstruct baseline current meaning from them when a canonical owner exists.

If a canonical document is incomplete or contradicts its historical source, that is a documentation defect to repair; it does not silently make the historical source disappear.

# Seam review

Extraction preserved the Phase 002/003 seams without contradiction:

* Coverage remains distinct from Aggregate;
* Rank remains derived and non-editable;
* one Judge × Encounter remains one logical Scorecard across versions/channels;
* current state remains distinct from historical observed state;
* Access remains distinct from semantic authority;
* Organizer/capture authority remains distinct from Judge authorship;
* paper/electronic channels preserve one evaluation model;
* calculated/ranking-ready/official remain distinct;
* official and published remain distinct;
* accessibility/resilience adaptations preserve meaning and authority;
* uncertain persistence never becomes false authoritative success.

No design refinement was required to complete extraction.

# Deliberate deferrals

004-C does not finalize:

* comprehensive source/provenance metadata on every historical phase record — 004-D;
* stable rule-ID namespace and broad restatement reduction — 004-E;
* canonical documentation/agent governance contracts — 004-F;
* full OKF generated/verified/lifecycle/freshness profile — 004-G;
* automated metadata/link/authority validation — 004-H.

Those later groups may refine metadata and references without changing the product meaning accepted here.

# Exit position

The repository can now answer ordinary current product/UX questions through `docs/index.md → canonical/ → category → subject` without loading the Phase 001–003 corpus.

004-C therefore passes extraction review and hands off to **004-D — Historical Phase Migration, Provenance & Source-Lineage Retrofit**.
