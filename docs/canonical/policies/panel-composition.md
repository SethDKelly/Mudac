# Canonical contract

Competition policy may define desired Panel composition capacities such as Academic, Business, and Technical.

A Judge's expertise is Participation metadata; the capacity they satisfy is Panel-specific. Baseline behavior assigns at most one required capacity per Judge per Panel while allowing at-large members where policy permits.

Composition status may be Compliant, Degraded, or Noncompliant. Explicit exceptions preserve the actual shortfall rather than pretending composition requirements were met. Such exceptions are scoped governed exceptions under [Operational Exception & Override Governance](operational-exception-governance.md#opg-001); they do not rewrite actual Panel/Encounter participation or waive unrelated evaluation/authorship constraints.

Final day-of-event staffing is operational readiness rather than a universal Competition Ready prerequisite.
