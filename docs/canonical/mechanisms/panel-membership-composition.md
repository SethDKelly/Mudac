# Canonical contract

Panel Membership relates a Judge [Participation](../concepts/participation.md) to a [Panel](../concepts/panel.md) for an effective interval and may record the composition capacity the Judge satisfies for that Panel.

Expertise is Participation metadata; composition capacity is Panel-specific use of that expertise. One Judge normally satisfies at most one required capacity for a Panel unless policy says otherwise.

Current membership never rewrites historical [Judging Encounter](../concepts/judging-encounter.md) participants.

See [Panel Composition Policy](../policies/panel-composition.md).
