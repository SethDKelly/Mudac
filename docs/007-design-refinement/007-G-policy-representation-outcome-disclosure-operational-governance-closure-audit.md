# Purpose

Establish the seventh evidence gate in the renewed Jackson-methodology runway by auditing MUDAC's **policy and representation plane as one integrated authority system**.

Earlier Phase 007 gates established the Concept catalog, synchronization authority, temporal/correction semantics, adversarial scenario closure, and UX-to-domain traceability. 007-G asks whether the remaining policy, result, disclosure, external-representation, exception, and operational-governance rules compose without contradiction or hidden authority.

The governing question is:

> Can MUDAC explain why evidence is eligible, when a shortfall may be exceptionally accepted, what result is calculated versus official, what information may be disclosed to which audience, which representation was released, and who had authority to make each consequential decision without relying on a generic override, mutable checklist, presentation shortcut, or technical-administrator power?

# Principal result

**007-G passes. The current sixteen-Concept system remains sufficient and the integrated policy/representation/outcome model closes without a new Concept.**

The audit finds no need for an `Outcome`, `Policy`, `Exception`, `Override`, `Decision`, `Disclosure`, `Incident`, or `Operational Governance` Concept.

Two canonical refinements are required:

1. **Operational exception/override governance requires one explicit cross-cutting policy owner.** Existing policy/mechanism documents correctly preserve shortfalls and semantic authority but distribute the general rule. 007-G establishes `Operational Exception & Override Governance` as a policy, not a Concept or generic ticket lifecycle.
2. **Representation truthfulness requires explicit source-authority constraints.** Export/Publication semantics already preserve exact source lineage and distribution history, but 007-G makes explicit that a representation cannot promote provisional/calculated/affected state into stronger authority than its source and that Publication prerequisites depend on the representation's purpose. Operational materials may legitimately be distributed before Competition Finalization; a representation claiming to communicate official Competition outcomes must bind to an identified Official Outcome Revision.

No existing Concept action is removed or repurposed.

# Integrated authority stack

The accepted model can be read as the following authority stack:

```text
Concept state/actions + immutable evidence
               ↓
policy constraints / explicit governed exceptions
               ↓
derived mechanisms
Coverage → Aggregate → Rank → readiness
               ↓
explicit Award decisions where applicable
               ↓
Competition Finalization
               ↓
Official Outcome Revision
               ↓
purpose + audience/disclosure profile
               ↓
Export bound to exact source basis
               ↓
explicit Publication of exact Export
               ↓
delivery/transport mechanisms
```

This diagram is not a mandatory linear workflow for every artifact. Operational Judge materials, paper Rubrics, event instructions, and other non-outcome representations may be exported or distributed earlier when their own source/lifecycle/disclosure requirements permit it.

What the diagram establishes is **authority monotonicity**: no downstream representation or operational mechanism may silently create stronger semantic authority than the source on which it is based.

# Policy role closure

Policies configure or constrain how Concepts and mechanisms compose. They do not own the underlying competition objects and do not become a generic state machine.

A policy can legitimately answer questions such as:

- which authoritative Scorecards are eligible;
- how many qualifying evaluations are required;
- what Rubric Versions are compatible;
- how ties are handled;
- which Panel composition capacities are expected;
- whether a declared shortfall may be accepted;
- which correction family is authorized;
- what conditions gate Finalization;
- what information may be disclosed to an audience;
- what capture-channel parity must be preserved.

A policy cannot silently:

- invent a Judge evaluation;
- rewrite an Encounter occurrence;
- convert missing evidence to zero;
- directly edit Aggregate or Rank;
- turn a calculated result into an official result;
- make an official result public;
- turn technical Administrator capability into Organizer/Judge semantic authority;
- erase an earlier Publication or disclosure occurrence.

**Result:** PASS.

# Evaluation Policy closure

Evaluation Policy correctly owns the outcome-affecting rules under which authoritative evidence becomes eligible for Coverage, Aggregate, and Rank.

The audit confirms:

- equal eligible individual Judge weighting remains the baseline and alternative weighting must be explicit policy;
- incompatible Rubric semantics cannot be silently pooled or normalized;
- outcome-affecting policy is reconstructible once judging begins;
- missing evaluation remains missing rather than numerical zero;
- aggregation and ranking consume authoritative eligible sources rather than presentation state;
- policy change after material evaluation activity cannot masquerade as an implementation configuration tweak.

A policy revision may legitimately change future or corrected calculations if authorized, but the official/public consequences remain separate. Recalculation does not itself establish a successor Official Outcome Revision.

**Result:** PASS.

# Coverage and exception closure

Coverage answers sufficiency, not performance.

A Team may have a meaningful numerical Aggregate while Coverage remains incomplete. `Exception Accepted` may change permission/eligibility where declared policy permits it, but the actual shortfall remains visible and reconstructible.

007-G closes the cross-policy rule:

```text
observed shortfall
      ↓
explicit exception authority + scope + reason
      ↓
permission/eligibility consequence

NOT

observed shortfall
      ↓
manual override
      ↓
pretend requirement was satisfied
```

An exception therefore operates **on the consequence of a known condition**, not on historical truth itself.

**Result:** PASS WITH CROSS-CUTTING POLICY OWNER.

# Panel composition closure

Panel composition policy distinguishes expertise metadata from Panel-specific composition capacity and permits Compliant, Degraded, or Noncompliant states.

An accepted composition exception:

- preserves the actual missing capacity/shortfall;
- does not relabel the Panel as objectively compliant;
- does not make one Judge count as multiple required perspectives unless policy explicitly defines such semantics;
- does not retroactively change who participated in completed Encounters;
- does not fabricate Scorecards;
- must be scoped to the policy consequence it is intended to affect.

Final day-of-event staffing remains an operational condition rather than a universal structural Competition Ready prerequisite.

**Result:** PASS.

# Correction authority closure

Correction policy remains semantic rather than role- or UI-based.

The accepted correction families remain distinct:

```text
working-state edit
semantic Judge amendment
capture/transcription correction
structural correction
provenance correction
official/public correction
```

No generic `edit anything` or `override` path is valid.

The audit reconfirms:

- only the semantic author may change Judge judgment through the legitimate amendment path;
- an Organizer may repair demonstrated transcription mismatch without becoming Judge author;
- structural errors change their owning relationship/occurrence or invoke invalidation/replacement rather than mutating Scorecard identity;
- post-Finalization correction leaves Competition Finalized;
- corrected calculations do not automatically displace the latest declared official revision;
- public correction requires explicit successor representation/publication behavior.

**Result:** PASS.

# Award and Finalization closure

Awards and Finalization remain two related but distinct authority areas.

For Awards:

- rank-derived Award candidates are constrained by ready Rank and declared selection semantics;
- Organizer confirmation cannot contradict the rule while retaining `rank-derived` meaning;
- discretionary Awards represent human judgment and must not be presented as mathematically implied;
- later Award correction preserves attributable history.

For Competition Finalization:

- Finalization is an explicit high-consequence Competition action;
- current evidence, Coverage/eligibility, Rubric compatibility, ranking readiness, required tie semantics, authoritative Evaluation Policy, required Award decisions, and unresolved outcome-affecting conditions are revalidated at execution time;
- successful Finalization establishes a reconstructible Official Outcome Revision;
- Finalization does not publish results.

No `finalization checklist complete` projection may substitute for `Competition.finalize` and its synchronization postconditions.

**Result:** PASS.

# Official Outcome Revision closure

Official Outcome Revision remains correctly modeled as a **derived/authority mechanism**, not a seventeenth Concept.

Its independent user purpose is insufficient to require a Concept because the meaningful user action is Competition Finalization or explicit successor official confirmation after reconciliation. The revision preserves the declared outcome basis created by that authority.

The mechanism correctly answers:

- what Ranking/Coverage/Award state MUDAC officially declared;
- under which Evaluation Policy and evidence basis;
- which official revision was current at a given time;
- whether the latest declared official revision has become Affected by later source correction;
- what successor later superseded it.

It does **not** replace Scorecards, Rank, Awards, Competition, Export, or Publication.

**Result:** PASS.

# Calculated → official boundary

007-G reconfirms the critical boundary:

```text
latest calculated Rank
      ≠
latest declared official Rank
```

A recalculation caused by late capture, correction, invalidation, policy correction, or replacement Encounter may change current computed state while the prior Official Outcome Revision remains the latest declared official revision and becomes Affected.

Only explicit successor confirmation changes official authority.

This prevents both automated accidental re-officialization and manual Organizer editing of Rank.

**Result:** PASS.

# Official → public boundary

The next boundary is equally strict:

```text
Official Outcome Revision
      ≠
Public disclosure
```

Finalization establishes internal official outcome authority. It does not imply that every official detail is appropriate for every audience.

Audience/purpose disclosure classification still applies before representation and Publication. For example, Judge Notes or individually attributable evaluation evidence can remain non-public even when the Competition outcome is official.

**Result:** PASS.

# Disclosure closure

Anonymity/disclosure policy remains coherent across interactive and external surfaces.

The Judge-facing blinded representation remains Alias + Division unless explicit current policy allows more. Organizer-sensitive visibility does not imply ceremony-safe or public visibility.

Disclosure applies to more than page bodies, including:

- search results;
- deep links;
- QR/encoded payloads;
- filenames and metadata;
- print;
- previews;
- Exports;
- Publications;
- accessible/nonvisual representations.

A representation can redact or omit information for its intended audience, but audience filtering does not permit semantic distortion of source authority or currentness.

An actual unauthorized disclosure remains historical even after future Access is revoked; resulting integrity consequences are assessed explicitly rather than erased or universalized automatically.

**Result:** PASS.

# Export closure

Export remains the correct owner of a stable representation tied to an exact identified source basis, purpose, audience/disclosure profile, and generation time.

007-G makes one truthfulness rule explicit:

> An Export may select, format, summarize, redact, or transform source information for an audience, but it may not silently make that source appear more authoritative, current, complete, or official than it actually is.

Examples:

- an Aggregate with incomplete Coverage cannot be represented as an official winner merely because a PDF was generated;
- a provisional Rank cannot be labeled Official without an Official Outcome Revision basis;
- an Affected historical official revision can be represented for audit/history, but the artifact must not silently present it as unaffected current truth;
- a Judge-safe representation may omit administrative identity but cannot change which Team/Division/Encounter it represents;
- regeneration after source correction creates a new Export rather than retargeting the old one.

**Result:** PASS WITH CANONICAL CLARIFICATION.

# Publication closure

Publication remains the deliberate act of making an exact Export available to a declared audience/channel.

007-G closes an important scope distinction:

> Publication does not universally require Competition Finalization. Its prerequisites depend on what the Export claims to represent.

Therefore:

- operational materials, event instructions, Judge-safe Rubrics, or other eligible non-outcome representations may be distributed before Finalization where their own source/lifecycle/disclosure policy permits;
- a Publication purporting to communicate **official Competition outcomes** must bind through its Export to an identified Official Outcome Revision;
- a Publication may not upgrade calculated/provisional results into official status;
- an earlier Publication remains historical even if later withdrawn or superseded;
- correction of source state never silently retargets an existing Publication to a newer Export;
- an existing Publication may temporarily remain Published while its source becomes Affected, but current presentation/distribution decisions must not conceal that consequence where material.

**Result:** PASS WITH CANONICAL CLARIFICATION.

# Publication versus delivery closure

Publication authority and transport success remain separate.

```text
publish exact Export
      ↓
Publication authority established
      ↓
delivery / CDN / print / QR / message transport
```

If transport fails after Publication authority was established, the domain fact that Publication occurred is not automatically rolled back. Recovery may retry delivery or withdraw/supersede under explicit Publication actions.

Conversely, a file being reachable through storage/CDN mechanics does not by itself establish legitimate Publication authority.

**Result:** PASS.

# Paper continuity closure

Paper remains a continuity channel, not an exception to policy or authority.

The audit confirms:

- paper and electronic evaluation use the same Rubric/evaluation semantics;
- Judge remains semantic author;
- Organizer capture remains capture/transcription activity;
- verification is required before paper-origin capture becomes authoritative;
- duplicate paper/electronic traces converge on one logical Scorecard;
- an operational outage does not lower disclosure, authorship, weighting, or evidence-eligibility standards;
- an exception cannot be used to guess ambiguous physical Judge intent.

Continuity policy therefore composes with Evaluation Policy and correction authority rather than overriding them.

**Result:** PASS.

# Operational-governance taxonomy

007-G distinguishes five conditions that implementations and UI must not collapse into one generic `override` or `resolve` action.

| Condition | Meaning | Legitimate consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warning | noteworthy condition that does not itself block authority | inform/guide; source action optional |
| Blocking precondition | required source/policy condition is false | action denied until source changes or a declared governed exception exists |
| Governed exception | explicit policy permits proceeding despite a preserved shortfall/condition | scoped permission/eligibility change with attribution/reason |
| Correction | current or historical source assertion is wrong or needs legitimate successor authority | owning correction/amendment/invalidation/replacement path |
| Technical emergency intervention | platform/session/infrastructure needs operational support | restore/restrict technical capability; never substitute semantic Competition authority |

This taxonomy is representational/governance knowledge, not a new domain lifecycle.

# Operational Exception & Override Governance

The new canonical policy owner establishes the general contract for any action described informally as exception, override, waiver, acknowledgement, suppression, escalation, or emergency approval.

The governing rule is:

```text
exception authority
    may alter a declared consequence
    of a known condition

exception authority
    may not alter the underlying fact
    merely to make the condition disappear
```

A governed exception must be:

- explicitly permitted by policy;
- scoped to the affected Competition/resource/policy condition;
- authorized by the correct semantic authority, not merely technical privilege;
- attributable to acting Identity/Participation and exceptional authorizer where different;
- reasoned where consequence is material;
- reconstructible enough to explain later official outcomes;
- limited to the declared permission/eligibility consequence;
- non-destructive to the observed shortfall/history.

# Non-overridable semantic invariants

Operational exception policy cannot be used as a generic escape hatch around MUDAC's core semantic invariants.

At minimum it cannot legitimately mean:

```text
missing evidence        → pretend zero or present
Organizer               → becomes Judge author
second capture          → second Judge vote
calculated Rank         → official Rank
official outcome        → automatically public
unknown command outcome → assume failure/success
invalidated evidence    → silently valid again
technical Administrator → Competition decision authority
historical Publication  → erase that release occurred
historical disclosure   → erase that exposure occurred
```

If a future requirement truly needs to alter one of these semantics, that is a design-policy change subject to canonical change governance—not an operational override.

# Acknowledgement and suppression closure

Operational users may need to acknowledge that they have seen a warning or temporarily suppress presentation noise. Such UI state is implementation/experience state unless a specific domain policy gives it semantic meaning.

`Acknowledge`, `dismiss`, `hide`, or `close` therefore does not mean:

- source corrected;
- Coverage satisfied;
- Panel compliant;
- Scorecard finalized;
- finalization gate cleared;
- disclosure approved;
- Publication withdrawn.

This closes the remaining seam identified by 007-F's exception-workspace audit.

# Technical administration closure

007-E already established that technical/system/break-glass power does not become Competition authority. 007-G confirms the consequence across policy and representation operations.

An Administrator or support operator may, where appropriately authorized operationally:

- restore service;
- revoke compromised sessions/access;
- inspect permitted diagnostics;
- execute infrastructure recovery mechanisms.

That position does not itself authorize the operator to:

- waive Coverage;
- accept a Panel composition exception;
- amend a Judge Scorecard;
- choose an Award recipient;
- finalize a Competition;
- establish a successor official outcome;
- override disclosure policy;
- publish Competition results.

If a technical mechanism facilitates an authorized semantic action, Provenance must preserve the true acting/represented authority distinctions.

**Result:** PASS.

# Policy precedence and contradiction closure

The audit rejects the idea that one policy document silently overrides another by being more convenient at execution time.

When multiple policies apply, their constraints compose. An exception changes only the specific consequence that its owning policy explicitly permits it to change.

Examples:

- accepting a Panel composition shortfall does not waive Scorecard authorship rules;
- accepting a Coverage exception does not make incompatible Rubric Versions compatible;
- Finalization authorization does not waive disclosure classification;
- Publication authority does not grant Access to unrelated private source evidence;
- continuity/paper fallback does not waive Evaluation Policy;
- technical emergency authority does not waive correction authority.

A real contradiction between canonical policies must return through change governance rather than being resolved by precedence-by-code-path.

**Result:** PASS.

# Current versus historical representation closure

007-D's temporal dimensions remain intact through 007-G.

The system must be able to represent simultaneously:

```text
current authoritative source
historical source state
latest calculated result
latest declared official revision
Affected official revision
current Export
historical/stale/affected Export
currently Published representation
withdrawn/superseded historical Publication
```

These dimensions are related but not interchangeable.

A current-results view may preferentially show the latest current/official/public state, while audit/history views must retain predecessors and explanatory lineage.

**Result:** PASS.

# Representational attack tests

007-G explicitly rejects these shortcuts:

1. generate a PDF from provisional Rank and label it `Official Results`;
2. change a result in a generated artifact instead of correcting source state;
3. treat an Organizer-only preview as permission to publish the same data publicly;
4. publish a newly generated corrected Export while silently pretending an earlier published artifact never existed;
5. leave a materially Affected result publicly presented as unquestionably current without current-state indication or governance decision;
6. encode protected Team/Judge information in filename, metadata, QR payload, or accessibility text while visually hiding it;
7. use a `Force Publish`/`Override` UI path to bypass disclosure validation;
8. use infrastructure reachability as evidence Publication authority exists;
9. use a withdrawn Publication to rewrite the historical fact that it had been released;
10. make a ceremony/public representation depend on mutable live derived state without an identified source basis.

The current model plus the 007-G clarifications reject all ten.

# Canonical changes from 007-G

007-G makes three focused current-authority changes.

## 1. Operational Exception & Override Governance policy

Adds a canonical policy owner for cross-cutting exception semantics, including:

- exceptions preserve observed source facts/shortfalls;
- exception authority is explicit, scoped, attributable, reasoned, and policy-permitted;
- acknowledgements/suppression do not establish resolution;
- exceptions do not transfer semantic authority;
- technical emergency capability does not grant policy-exception authority;
- core invariants cannot be bypassed through a generic override.

## 2. Export truthfulness

Adds a stable Export rule stating that audience transformation may not promote source authority/currentness/completeness. Representation cannot turn calculated/provisional/Affected state into stronger authority through formatting or labeling.

## 3. Publication purpose/source authority

Adds a stable Publication rule stating that publication prerequisites are purpose/source-specific. Operational materials need not wait for Competition Finalization, but a representation claiming official Competition outcomes must bind to an identified Official Outcome Revision and cannot promote provisional calculation into official authority.

# Implementation-freeze consequence

007-G is design work only.

It does **not** authorize implementation of:

- policy engines or generic rule DSLs;
- exception/waiver database tables;
- override APIs;
- Administrator control panels;
