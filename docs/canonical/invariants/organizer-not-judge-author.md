# Invariant

Organizer/Admin capability to view, coordinate, capture, recover, invalidate, or correct process state does not make that actor the author of Judge judgment.

Paper transcription preserves Judge as evaluation author and Organizer as capture actor. Judge judgment changes require Judge amendment or another explicit authority path; Organizer cannot infer them.

See [Provenance](../concepts/provenance.md) and [Correction & Authority](../policies/correction-authority.md).
