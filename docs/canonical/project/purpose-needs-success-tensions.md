# Purpose of this document

Provide the current need-focused purpose baseline for MUDAC after 010-C.

This document states **why the product matters and what outcomes it must enable**. It does not define the final Concept catalog. The current sixteen Concepts remain an incumbent hypothesis until candidate rediscovery, specification and modularity review are complete.

# Product-level purpose

MUDAC should enable a live student data competition to turn **independent human judgments into fair, explainable and correctable competition outcomes under real event conditions**, while keeping administrative/technical burden secondary to judging and preventing identity bias, operational failure, technical privilege or external representation from silently changing what was judged or what is authoritative.

# Actor and affected-party needs

## Judge

A Judge needs to:

- know the correct judging context and declared evaluation basis;
- form and record an independent judgment without peer-score, standings or protected-identity influence;
- retain authorship even when paper capture is performed by someone else;
- participate with low administrative friction through accessible interaction;
- preserve legitimate work across ordinary interruption or degraded connectivity;
- distinguish preserved work from asserted/authoritative evaluation;
- understand what judging work remains during the event;
- amend the Judge's own evaluation through a legitimate correction path;
- avoid unnecessary broad post-event exposure of private evaluation history.

## Organizer

An Organizer needs to:

- establish the competition-specific judging context and evaluation basis;
- prepare evaluators/competitors and coordinate live judging;
- see completion, gaps, substitutions and exceptions quickly;
- preserve missing evidence as missing rather than inventing a score;
- recover from paper operation, no-shows, interruption and mistakes without inventing judgment;
- determine whether evaluation evidence satisfies declared rules;
- derive, reconcile and declare outcomes from qualifying evidence under explicit policy;
- distinguish live/calculated information from official outcomes;
- correct legitimate errors without destroying prior historical truth;
- explain outcomes from their underlying evidence and rules;
- externalize information without silently changing its authority/currentness;
- deliberately govern disclosure/release.

## Technical administrator / support operator

A technical operator needs to keep the environment usable and secure without technical capability silently becoming Judge or Organizer semantic authority or falsifying represented authorship.

## Student Team

A Student Team needs:

- evaluation based on legitimate competition information rather than protected institutional identity or administrative accident;
- missing evaluation to remain distinguishable from a low score;
- sufficiently consistent application of declared evaluation/eligibility rules;
- traceable outcomes and historically honest correction;
- provisional information to remain distinguishable from official outcomes.

Student Teams remain materially affected non-users in the current baseline.

## External recipient

A recipient of printed or deliberately released material needs an honest representation of its identified source basis, disclosure intent and authority/currentness. A rich public application is not required for this need.

# Current purpose obligations

## P-01 — Independent human judgment

A Judge can form, record and assert the Judge's own evaluation without peer scoring, standings, protected identity or capture mechanics substituting for that judgment.

## P-02 — Fair and bias-aware Team treatment

A Team's competitive treatment reflects legitimate evidence and declared competition distinctions rather than protected institutional identity, missing-data artifacts, administrative accident or opaque arbitrary ordering.

## P-03 — Low-friction and accessible participation

Volunteers can enter the correct competition context and perform authorized judging work with low administrative overhead through accessible interaction paths, without convenience erasing trustworthy attribution or authority boundaries.

## P-04 — Live operational coordination and completion

Organizers can prepare and operate the time-bounded event, understand readiness/incompletion, handle substitutions and exceptions, and determine remaining work without ad-hoc manual reconstruction.

## P-05 — Resilient evaluation continuity

Interruption, connectivity loss, device failure or paper operation does not unnecessarily destroy legitimate work, duplicate semantic judgment, change authorship or make uncertain state appear authoritative.

## P-06 — Trustworthy and explainable outcome formation

Organizers can derive, reconcile and declare outcomes from qualifying individual judgments and explicit rules while distinguishing incomplete, calculated/provisional and official information. Declared outcomes remain explainable through their evidence and rules.

## P-07 — Correctable authority and historical truth

Legitimate amendments, invalidations, replacements and corrections can improve current authoritative truth without destructive rewrite, silent predecessor revival, authorship transfer or loss of prior authority history.

## P-08 — Contextual confidentiality and authority separation

People can see and do what current competition context legitimately permits, while protected identity, private evaluation and semantic competition authority do not leak through technical privilege, stale access, shared devices or prior participation.

## P-09 — Faithful external representation and controlled release

Printable or externally released material accurately represents identified source state and disclosure intent, does not elevate provisional information into official truth, and is released/withdrawn under deliberate competition authority.

# Representative success situations

Current success is scenario-verifiable; 010-C found no evidence supporting invented numeric KPI thresholds.

- **S-01:** a first-time Judge can enter the correct context and complete an independent evaluation without protected/peer information leakage.
- **S-02:** a no-show or incomplete evaluation remains visibly missing rather than becoming zero or silently complete.
- **S-03:** a Judge substitution restores operational capacity without rewriting earlier actual participation or authorship.
- **S-04:** routine interruption does not unnecessarily destroy entered work or falsely report uncertain authority.
- **S-05:** paper-originated judging remains substantively equivalent, Judge-authored and separately capture-attributed.
- **S-06:** an Organizer can identify unresolved obligations and exceptions during live operation.
- **S-07:** an official result can be traced to qualifying evaluation evidence and declared rules.
- **S-08:** correction can change current authority while preserving the prior record and meaningful reason/source history.
- **S-09:** ordinary Judge access to private evaluation history can expire without deleting Organizer-authorized historical evidence.
- **S-10:** technical support can recover the system without silently acquiring competition decision authority.
- **S-11:** calculated/provisional information cannot become official merely because it is displayed, printed or exported.
- **S-12:** corrected external material uses explicit successor representation/release rather than silent historical mutation.

# Material tensions

## T-01 — Judge friction vs trustworthy attribution

Reduce event-day friction without allowing convenience to substitute for trustworthy actor/context continuity.

## T-02 — Identity shielding vs explainability

Audience-specific disclosure may differ; authorized historical/evidentiary reconstruction must remain possible.

## T-03 — Judge privacy vs Organizer oversight

Operational need should grant only legitimate visibility rather than normalize broad disclosure.

## T-04 — Resilience/multi-channel capture vs singular logical authority

Multiple capture/continuity paths must not accidentally multiply semantic judgment authority.

## T-05 — Live-event speed vs outcome correctness

Progress visibility can be fast; official authority must remain gated by qualifying evidence and policy.

## T-06 — Organizer exception handling vs consistent Team treatment

Exceptions must preserve the source condition, actor/reason and explicitly permitted consequence rather than pretending the exception never occurred.

## T-07 — Correctability vs historical immutability

Correction should establish new current authority through explicit successor/invalidation/replacement semantics rather than destructive rewrite.

## T-08 — Accessibility/continuity vs confidentiality/security

Accessible and degraded-operation paths remain first-class without making privacy or authority optional.

## T-09 — Live visibility vs independent judgment

Information useful for Organizer operation does not imply that Judges should receive score/outcome visibility.

## T-10 — External transparency vs controlled disclosure/currentness

External availability must preserve source basis, disclosure contract and historical release truth; release cannot create stronger source authority.

# Distributional guardrails

- Organizer convenience must not turn missing evaluation into zero or substitute Organizer authorship for Judge authorship.
- Judge convenience must not make identity shielding, attribution or correction impossible.
- Technical-operator convenience must not collapse platform privilege into competition authority.
- Fast live ranking must not shift incompleteness/reconciliation risk onto Teams by presenting provisional evidence as final.
- External transparency must not shift privacy or stale-information risk onto Judges or Teams without deliberate disclosure authority.
- Digital efficiency must not exclude Judges who need accessible or paper-supported participation.

# Candidate-rediscovery pressure

010-C found no wholly orphaned product-level purpose, but current coverage does **not** prove the incumbent catalog is complete or correctly factored.

The strongest 010-D pressure is:

- **P-03:** accessibility/low-friction participation is distributed across current identity/participation/access and experience knowledge; revisit onboarding/enrollment/recovery candidates without promoting authentication mechanisms by default.
- **P-04:** live coordination is distributed across competition, participation, grouping, judging-occurrence and readiness/reconciliation knowledge; test different decompositions.
- **P-05:** continuity spans judgment state, paper capture, history, privacy and experience; revisit `Recovery`/`Continuity` from purpose first.
- **P-06:** major outcome responsibilities currently live in non-Concept mechanisms such as Coverage, Aggregate, Rank, Reconciliation and Official Outcome Revision; actively test their Concept candidacy.
- **P-07:** Versioning/Provenance strongly serve correction/history but separate Concept status remains open.
- **P-09:** external representation/release is a real purpose, while the current Export/Publication split still requires behavioral justification.

Current Concepts with especially important boundary pressure include `Competition`, `Division`, `Panel`, `Judging Encounter`, `Identity`, `Participation`, `Alias`, `Access`, `Versioning`, `Provenance`, `Export` and `Publication`. This is not a rejection list.

# Interpretation rule

Purpose traceability means only that current design knowledge can be related to a valued outcome.

It does **not** establish that a current Concept is:

- necessary;
- specific;
- complete;
- independent;
- familiar;
- generically reusable;
- correctly named;
- correctly split/merged;
- required in every MUDAC variant.

Those questions belong to 010-D onward and later methodology phases.
