# Purpose

Perform the final adversarial/methodology audit of Phase 012 before consolidation.

012-J does not select another product variant and does not reopen already-settled Concept edges merely because a different interface or workflow could be imagined.

It asks four questions:

1. Does a counterexample invalidate any accepted dependence, capability rule, or PF-01 scope decision?
2. Does any finding require reopening Phase 010 Concept/purpose work or Phase 011 composition?
3. What explanation-order implications are legitimate consequences of the conceptual model, without turning the dependence DAG into navigation or workflow order?
4. What precise current authority and mapping-risk handoff must Phase 013 receive?

# 2. Counterexample discipline

A useful counterexample must challenge a specific claim.

012-J distinguishes:

```text
counterexample to direct dependence
counterexample to capability-conditioned inclusion
counterexample to product-scope selection
counterexample to composition
counterexample to mapping/representation
```

Do not treat a mapping inconvenience as proof of a Concept defect, and do not treat a coherent but unadopted contraction as proof that PF-01 scope is invalid.

# 3. Counterexample audit matrix

## CEX-01 — PF-01 contains all eighteen Concepts, therefore Competition depends on all eighteen

**Claim challenged:** scope might have silently collapsed optionality back into universal dependence.

**Result: rejected.**

PF-01 product scope means the product supports the capabilities owned by all eighteen Concepts. It does not mean every Competition instance uses every capability or that `Competition → every Concept` exists.

The direct graph remains unchanged.

## CEX-02 — A Competition with no Award proves Award should not be in PF-01

**Result: rejected.**

Award is supported capability and event-optional recognition.

```text
Award capability in product scope
  != Award instance/conferral required in every Competition
```

No product variant split is needed.

## CEX-03 — An official result that is not published proves Export/Publication are separate product variants

**Result: rejected.**

Official-but-non-public is an in-scope PF-01 operating profile.

```text
official != public
```

Outcome Declaration may exist without Export/Publication, while PF-01 still supports externalization when needed.

## CEX-04 — An Export prepared without Publication proves Publication is not part of PF-01

**Result: rejected.**

Prepared-but-unreleased representation is an in-scope state/profile.

`Publication → Export` remains valid; the reverse does not.

## CEX-05 — A Competition before Outcome Declaration is a judging-only product

**Result: rejected.**

Event Completed before ordinary closeout is a lifecycle state inside PF-01.

A completed **product contract** that permanently stops at judging is a different coherent contraction and remains unadopted.

Lifecycle progression must not be mistaken for product-family branching.

## CEX-06 — Ad-hoc Judge substitution means Panel is unnecessary in PF-01

**Result: rejected.**

One-off actual occurrence participation and live substitution do not erase reusable Panel grouping capability.

PF-01 may perform an ad-hoc operational change while retaining Panel as a supported organizational Concept.

## CEX-07 — A single competitive cohort means Division should be removed from PF-01

**Result: rejected for current scope; future contraction remains coherent.**

Current PF-01 uses Division in current blinded presentation and Rank semantics.

A truly no-Division product remains dependence-coherent but requires explicit policy/composition generalization before adoption.

Do not fabricate a meaningless placeholder Division; equally, do not treat one legitimate competition cohort as evidence that Division capability is globally unnecessary.

## CEX-08 — Bias can sometimes be acceptable, so Alias should be optional in ordinary PF-01 judging

**Result: rejected under current purpose/scope.**

The project purpose explicitly protects bias-sensitive Team identity during ordinary judging.

An intentionally non-blinded competitive variant is a possible future purpose/policy change, not a current PF-01 mapping choice.

## CEX-09 — Access has no direct dependence edge, so it should not be in PF-01

**Result: rejected.**

Independent inclusion is not exclusion.

Access has no universal `Access → Participation/Identity` edge because it has broader contextual roles, but protected Judge/Organizer action and disclosure control are explicit PF-01 capabilities.

This is a useful demonstration that product scope cannot be reconstructed from outgoing graph edges alone.

## CEX-10 — Versioning and Provenance have no universal Rubric/Scorecard edges, so authoritative PF-01 can omit them

**Result: invalid.**

The authority capability rules remain:

```text
Authoritative Rubric Basis
  ⇒ Versioning + Provenance

Authoritative Scorecard Evidence
  ⇒ Versioning + Provenance
```

PF-01 claims authoritative/correctable evaluation, so both support Concepts remain in scope.

Working non-authoritative states do not contradict this.

## CEX-11 — Evaluation Obligation without Occurrence proves ordinary PF-01 synchronization is over-constrained

**Result: rejected as an upstream defect.**

012-D/H established the contraction as coherent, but 012-I did not adopt it as a standalone product.

Ordinary PF-01 may still synchronize occurrence begin with responsibility establishment. The existence of another coherent product contraction does not invalidate current full-product composition.

## CEX-12 — Evaluation Occurrence without Obligation proves responsibility should be removed

**Result: rejected.**

Occurrence history and responsibility are independent owners. PF-01 needs both capabilities for current live judging and operational completion.

An occurrence may legitimately exist before obligations are established; that state already demonstrates the distinction without requiring a separate product.

## CEX-13 — A no-result official declaration proves Outcome Declaration needs direct Team/evaluation edges

**Result: rejected.**

An official exceptional/no-result disposition may be based on a reconstructible accepted OutcomeBasis without fabricating normal ranked Team result content.

Therefore fixed direct edges such as:

```text
Outcome Declaration → Team
Outcome Declaration → Scorecard
Outcome Declaration → Evaluation Obligation
```

remain unjustified.

## CEX-14 — Public non-official material proves Publication should depend directly on Competition/Outcome Declaration

**Result: rejected.**

Publication's universal MUDAC dependency remains Export.

Legitimate public non-official material is a counterexample to `Publication → Outcome Declaration`.

PF-01 family scope separately retains Competition.

## CEX-15 — Paper operation proves Export is mandatory for judging

**Result: rejected.**

Physical source/capture continuity is not the same as stable external representation.

Paper evidence can be preserved, verified and captured under Scorecard/Versioning/Provenance semantics without requiring Export merely because paper exists.

## CEX-16 — Source correction after Finalization should reopen Competition or silently replace official/public state

**Result: rejected.**

Current semantics deliberately preserve:

```text
Competition remains Finalized
current declaration may become Affected
corrected derived state is not official automatically
successor declaration requires explicit authority
existing Publication is not silently retargeted
```

No missing Concept or synchronization is exposed.

## CEX-17 — Award → Competition is redundant because Award → Team → Competition

**Result: no change.**

The edge remains reachability-redundant but semantically retained:

```text
Award → Competition = recognition scope
Award → Team        = recipient type
```

The counterexample does not erase the independent scope reason.

## CEX-18 — PF-01 is one product, therefore all mapped experiences should be one generic role/workflow

**Result: rejected.**

One product variant can contain materially different actor perspectives, authority contexts, work modes, temporal states and application actions.

Phase 013 must map Judge, Organizer, support operator and affected-party questions without creating a generic `User` workflow that hides authority distinctions.

# 5. Mapping-currentness audit

The pre-existing canonical Experience corpus contains useful design evidence, but some records still encode terminology/ownership that is no longer current.

Examples observed during 012-J include:

- `context-role-modes.md` says Judge experience centers on a current `Encounter`;
- `judge-evaluation.md` asks the Judge to resolve/confirm an `Encounter`;
- `live-operations.md` uses `Encounter` state and one-off `Encounter` substitution language;
- `status-feedback-recovery.md` uses `Encounter Complete` as a finality example;
- `action-authority-traceability.md` contains an `Encounter`-centered Judge chain and points readers toward the archival `concept-synchronizations.md` adapter;
- `reconciliation-finalization.md` says success establishes an `Official Outcome Revision`, which Phase 010 superseded with Outcome Declaration.

These are not merely cosmetic word substitutions.

For example, one old `Encounter` label can hide the important current distinction:

```text
Evaluation Occurrence
  != Evaluation Obligation
```

Likewise `Official Outcome Revision` can hide:

```text
Outcome Declaration
  = explicit Concept-owned official authority/currentness/history
```

Phase 013 must therefore revalidate meaning, not run a blind search-and-replace.

# 7. Explanation order — what dependence legitimately implies

Dependence and synchronization constrain what must be understandable, but they do not prescribe screen order, navigation, route order, or a wizard.

The governing rule is:

> **Explain enough upstream context/basis/authority for the downstream state or action to be interpreted correctly; do not mechanically render the dependence DAG as the interaction sequence.**

# 8. Explanation-order layers

The following is a semantic explanation order, not a page order.

## EO-01 — Scope and perspective before ambiguous state

When meaning depends on context, representation must make enough of the following intelligible before or with the downstream state/action:

```text
Competition
actor perspective / Participation context
Team subject
applicable Division/Alias disclosure context
```

Do not assume a user knows which Competition, role, Team or disclosure context a value/action belongs to merely because navigation previously selected it.

## EO-02 — Evaluation basis/context before judgment commitment

A Judge must be able to understand enough of:

```text
Evaluation Occurrence context
Evaluation Obligation responsibility where applicable
Rubric / exact evaluation basis
Team Judge-facing context
```

before committing authoritative judgment.

This does not require showing internal IDs or all composition participants.

## EO-03 — Working state before authority claim

Representation must preserve:

```text
Draft / preserved work
  != authoritative Scorecard evidence
```

and when authoritative lineage matters:

```text
logical Scorecard
current authoritative Version
Provenance / represented authorship
```

must not be visually or linguistically collapsed.

## EO-04 — Evidence before derived interpretation

Where Organizers reason about outcome state, mapping must preserve the direction:

```text
eligible authoritative evidence
  → Coverage / Aggregate
  → rank eligibility / Rank
```

without presenting a derived value as though it were editable source truth.

## EO-05 — Calculated, recognized and official are separate

Mapping must preserve:

```text
calculated != recognized != official
```

Therefore:

- Rank does not imply Award;
- Award does not imply official declaration;
- current calculations do not imply Outcome Declaration;
- Outcome Declaration does not imply Publication.

## EO-06 — Source authority before representation/release claims

For externalization:

```text
source authority/currentness
  → Export representation/currentness
  → Publication release state
  → downstream delivery realization
```

must remain distinguishable.

The representation may present these layers together, but cannot collapse them into one generic `Published result` status when the distinction changes decisions.

## EO-07 — Current/historical/corrected meaning before corrective action

Before correction/replacement/successor actions, users must understand whether the target is:

- current;
- historical;
- invalidated;
- superseded;
- affected/stale;
- replaced;
- awaiting explicit successor authority.

Correction actions must not be mapped as generic edit/delete when their semantics preserve historical truth.

# 9. Explanation order is not navigation order

012-J explicitly rejects:

```text
dependence edge order
  == screen sequence
```

and:

```text
synchronization chain
  == mandatory wizard
```

A mapping may surface a downstream question first—for example an Organizer may begin from an exception or an official declaration—but it must provide enough traceability/context to avoid a false mental model.

Examples:

- an outcome view may lead with official status while still exposing its exact OutcomeBasis;
- a live-operations view may lead with outstanding responsibility while allowing drill-through to occurrence/context;
- a Publication view may lead with release state while preserving exact Export/source identity;
- a Judge task may lead with the current Team/Rubric task without exposing the internal dependence graph.

# 10. Phase-013 mapping risk hotspots

012-J identifies the following high-priority mapping risks for Phase 013-A planning.

## MR-01 — Deprecated `Encounter` language

Risk: collapse Evaluation Occurrence and Evaluation Obligation back into one mental model.

## MR-02 — Deprecated `Official Outcome Revision` language

Risk: demote explicit Outcome Declaration authority to a generic revision artifact.

## MR-03 — Application action versus Concept action

Risk: expose composition-only participant actions as user-facing generic controls or hide coordinated consequences.

Phase 013 must map the **Phase-011 application action surface**, not the union of every Concept action.

## MR-04 — Identity / Participation / Access collapse

Risk: role/navigation context appearing to create authority or union capabilities.

## MR-05 — Panel / actual participant / responsibility / evidence collapse

Risk: membership or schedule presentation implying actual evaluation participation, responsibility or Scorecard evidence.

## MR-06 — Draft / Finalized / authoritative lineage collapse

Risk: persistence/completion feedback being mistaken for authority.

## MR-07 — Missing / zero / incomplete / exception collapse

Risk: operational convenience altering evidence meaning or Team outcome.

## MR-08 — Rank / Award / official declaration collapse

Risk: derived ordering or recognition being presented as official authority.

## MR-09 — official / public / delivered collapse

Risk: Outcome Declaration, Export, Publication and delivery appearing to be one state.

## MR-10 — correction/history flattening

Risk: amendment, invalidation, replacement, affectedness, supersession and withdrawal appearing as ordinary edit/delete.

## MR-11 — profile versus product-variant confusion

Risk: Award omission, paper capture, unpublished official outcome or successor state being represented as a different product variant.

PF-01 is the sole adopted product variant entering Phase 013.

## MR-12 — accessibility/context loss

Risk: compact/mobile/nonvisual/degraded/paper mappings dropping authority, target, currentness or consequence distinctions.
