# 5. Purpose-compatible integrity boundaries

The following limitations/boundaries were surfaced repeatedly and remain **compatible with current purpose**.

They are not unresolved defects.

## LIM-01 — Controlled blinding, not absolute anonymity

Alias protects the relevant Judge-facing identity context but does not promise permanent non-resolvability to all authorized actors.

Scenario pressure remains useful for accidental/selective disclosure.

## LIM-02 — Responsibility may outlive occurrence/live-event completion

An existing Evaluation Obligation may remain legitimately actionable after the bounded event/live phase ends when current policy and fresh Access permit the same logical work.

This does not reactivate Participation or recreate broad event-day authority.

## LIM-03 — Capture Actor may differ from Judge semantic author

Paper/assisted/source-faithful capture may distinguish Actor, RepresentedAuthority and Source.

This remains valid only when the Judge's content/intent is supported without inference.

## LIM-04 — Owner-local currentness can differ from composed eligibility

A retained authoritative Scorecard Version may remain authentic/current within its lineage while becoming ineligible for a current downstream purpose because a dependency changed.

## LIM-05 — Factual Coverage can remain Incomplete while policy permits a consequence

An accepted exception may permit ranking/closeout where policy allows without rewriting Coverage truth.

## LIM-06 — Affected official authority remains official pending explicit successor

An Outcome Declaration may remain the latest declared authority while known to require correction/review.

New calculations do not silently become official.

## LIM-07 — Export currency is source/use relative

Export Current/Affected/Stale/Superseded/Retired do not redefine source authority or Publication state.

## LIM-08 — Withdrawal/supersession cannot recall external copies

Current MUDAC release authority may end while downstream possession persists.

## LIM-09 — Profile disclosure may differ while source truth stays one

Judge, Organizer, support, public and history views may legitimately expose different projections under policy without creating different domain truth.

## LIM-10 — Degraded operation may safely reduce capability

When current Access, basis, prior-result truth or other prerequisites cannot be established, an operation may be unavailable rather than replaced by a weaker-authority shortcut.

## LIM-11 — Uncertainty is first-class

Pending/unknown authority outcome is neither success nor failure.

Recovery must reconcile truth before new semantic effects are inferred.

## LIM-12 — One coordinated intent may have multiple owner-specific effects

A user-facing coordinated action may legitimately establish several independent postconditions when those effects are part of the accepted semantic action.

The application must not imply that one hidden coordinator owns those states.

These boundaries should be stressed in Phase 016 because contextual use may still reveal a misfit even though the structure itself is coherent.

# 6. Phase-016 target-preparation rule

Base/Jackson Phase 010 / MUDAC Phase 016 is scenario-driven rather than test-driven.

A 015-J target seed is appropriate only when:

- current structure is coherent;
- the concern depends on contextual conditions, human behavior, mistake, timing, misuse, recovery, privacy, affected-party perspective or adverse incentives;
- a scenario can test a concrete purpose/promise;
- the target does not hide a known Phase-015 contradiction;
- no implementation mechanism is required to state the conceptual challenge.

The seeds below are **inputs to 016-A**, not a fixed Phase-016 B–X plan.

016-A may merge, split, reprioritize or discard a seed when it derives the risk/context coverage plan.

# 7. Phase-016 scenario target seed register

## SVT-01 — Representative end-to-end blinded judging success

**Family:** representative success.

Trace ordinary PF-01 success from:

```text
Competition preparation
→ Judge Participation/context
→ Panel / Evaluation Occurrence
→ Evaluation Obligation
→ exact Rubric basis
→ Scorecard Draft + explicit Finalization
→ Coverage/Aggregate/Rank
→ optional Award
→ Finalize Competition & Declare Outcome
→ optional Export
→ optional Publication
```

Purpose:

- demonstrate that later correction/genericity/integrity refinements did not make the central use case incoherent;
- exercise P-01 through P-09 at least representatively.

Potential misfit:

> central success now requires a hidden semantic shortcut or unexplained owner transition.

**Priority:** mandatory Phase-016 coverage.

## SVT-02 — Multi-capacity actor, stale context and disclosure leakage

**Families:** authority, privacy/disclosure, mistake, affected-party.

Context:

- one Identity legitimately has Judge and Organizer Participations;
- actor switches contexts;
- stale route/view/device state remains;
- organizer-sensitive Team identity or outcome state was previously visible.

Challenge:

> Can the actor accidentally or strategically carry Organizer disclosure/capability into Judge context, or act from stale context while believing it is current?

Promises stressed:

- Identity != Participation != Access;
- no capability union;
- Alias + Division Judge-safe disclosure;
- stale route/session/device != authority.

Potential result classes:

- validated;
- mapping/privacy misfit;
- accepted contextual boundary;
- downstream engineering concern after conceptual semantics validate.

## SVT-03 — Event Completed with unfinished Judge responsibility

**Families:** partial completion, temporal, authority/revocation, recovery.

Context:

- live event reaches Event Completed;
- Judge Participation completes;
- one legitimate obligation remains Outstanding;
- Draft may or may not exist;
- current Access changes or must be re-established.

Challenge:

> Can the Judge continue only the same legitimate responsibility without broad authority reactivation, and is the distinction intelligible?

Promises stressed:

- occurrence/event completion != responsibility satisfaction;
- completed Participation != restored active authority;
- fresh Access gates current capability;
- one logical Scorecard remains.

## SVT-04 — Panel shortage, recusal/absence and governed exception

**Families:** exception, fairness, affected-party, policy.

Context:

- planned Panel composition is not realized;
- one or more Judges are absent/recused;
- actual occurrence starts with a degraded evaluator set;
- resulting Coverage is or later becomes incomplete;
- Organizer considers permitted exception.

Challenge:

> Does MUDAC preserve factual shortfall while allowing only explicitly governed consequences, without inventing participants/evidence or unfairly converting missing to zero?

Promises stressed:

- Panel != occurrence participation;
- missing != zero;
- Coverage fact != exception consequence;
- Team treatment remains explainable/consistent.

## SVT-05 — Paper/electronic conflict and ambiguous represented authority

**Families:** recovery, correction, uncertainty, authority.

Context:

- paper Judge source exists;
- electronic Draft or prior trace also exists;
- transcription/capture Actor differs from Judge;
- source content or Finalization intent is ambiguous/conflicting.

Challenge:

> Can the design preserve one logical Scorecard and Judge authorship without guessing intent, duplicating vote weight or treating capture Actor as author?

Promises stressed:

- Actor / RepresentedAuthority / Source;
- one logical Scorecard;
- paper/electronic parity;
- ambiguity remains ambiguity.

## SVT-06 — Structural misbinding discovered after Finalization

**Families:** correction, temporal/history, recovery.

Context:

- Scorecard was authoritatively finalized;
- later evidence shows wrong Evaluator, Team, OccurrenceContext or EvaluationBasis binding;
- historical obligation was Satisfied;
- current evidence becomes unusable.

Challenge:

> Does invalidation preserve authentic history while preventing silent rebinding, predecessor revival or automatic successor work?

Promises stressed:

- structural identity is not ordinary edit;
- historical satisfaction != current eligibility;
- invalidation != supersession != replacement;
- successor responsibility is deliberate.

## SVT-07 — Strategic missingness / recusal / delay intended to influence outcomes

**Families:** strategic misuse/incentive, fairness, exception.

Context:

- a Judge strategically delays, refuses, recuses late or otherwise withholds qualifying evidence while remaining within ordinary conceptual action boundaries;
- missing evidence changes Coverage/rank eligibility;
- Organizer faces an exception decision.

Challenge:

> Can a participant manipulate competitive outcome or force favorable exception treatment through selective non-participation/missingness?

Promises stressed:

- P-02 fair Team treatment;
- Coverage truth;
- exception authority;
- Judge responsibility;
- no automatic zero/fabricated evidence.

This target does not presume punitive policy. It tests whether current semantics leave a consequential incentive misfit.

## SVT-08 — Post-closeout correction with unchanged visible winner

**Families:** temporal/history, correction, authority, familiarity.

Context:

- Competition Finalized;
- Outcome Declaration Current;
- Award/results are official;
- a material evidence/basis correction occurs;
- recomputed visible winner/ranking happens to remain unchanged.

Challenge:

> Do actors incorrectly treat same visible result as proof that the existing declaration remains Current?

Promises stressed:

- same visible result != same declared basis;
- Competition remains Finalized;
- declaration becomes Affected when materially dependent;
- explicit successor confirmation required.

## SVT-09 — Correction after public release and irreversible external possession

**Families:** correction, privacy/disclosure, recovery, affected-party, irreversible consequence.

Context:

- official outcome represented and Published;
- recipients downloaded/printed/shared the artifact;
- source later becomes Affected/Stale and corrected;
- MUDAC considers withdrawal/successor release.

Challenge:

> Can current authority be corrected honestly without implying that external copies disappeared or automatically republishing?

Promises stressed:

- source != Export != Publication != possession;
- withdrawal != recall;
- successor declaration != successor Export != successor Publication;
- affected parties can understand current release status.

## SVT-10 — Unknown result and repeated high-consequence intent

**Families:** uncertainty, recovery, user mistake.

Exercise at least one high-consequence action such as:

- Finalize Evaluation;
- Finalize Competition & Declare Outcome;
- Confirm Successor Outcome Declaration;
- Publish Representation.

Context:

- action was attempted;
- authoritative result is unknown;
- actor repeats intent or resumes on another device/context.

Challenge:

> Does the conceptual experience reconcile current truth first and converge on the same intended semantic result rather than intentionally duplicating authority?

Promises stressed:

- unknown != success != failure;
- repeated intent != new semantic object by default;
- stale local state cannot overwrite current authority.

Runtime idempotency/transactions remain downstream.

## SVT-11 — Exceptional or no-result official disposition

**Families:** exception, domain misfit, representative alternative success.

Context:

- qualifying evidence/ranking cannot produce an ordinary result;
- Competition still requires legitimate closeout;
- Award may be absent;
- ordinary ranking must not be fabricated.

Challenge:

> Can Outcome Declaration express a legitimate exceptional/no-result official disposition with a reconstructible accepted basis while preserving missing/exception truth?

Promises stressed:

- official outcome authority does not require fabricated Rank/Award;
- PF-01 supports exceptional/no-result disposition;
- calculated/recognized/official remain distinct.

## SVT-12 — Familiarity trap under consequential action

**Families:** familiarity expectation, user mistake, mapping.

Use plausible user expectations around high-risk words such as:

```text
Submit
Final
Winner
Share
Published
Reopen
Delete
```

Challenge:

> Would a plausible user predict the wrong semantic consequence because familiar vocabulary is simplified in an adverse or edge context?

Examples:

- assuming `Submit` means Scorecard Finalized;
- assuming `Competition Finalized` means public official result;
- assuming top-ranked Team is already recognized winner;
- assuming `Published` means delivered/current;
- assuming `Withdrawn` means copies disappeared.

Promises stressed:

- qualified vocabulary;
- owner-specific action/state meaning;
- retained history/currentness.

## SVT-13 — Degraded/offline/shared-device privacy and capability reduction

**Families:** degraded operation, privacy, interruption/recovery, accessibility.

Context may combine:

- intermittent/no connectivity;
- local Draft state;
- stale protected view;
- device handoff;
- QR/deep-link possession;
- accessibility alternative input.

Challenge:

> When prerequisites cannot be safely re-established, does the design reduce capability without leaking protected information or silently lowering authority requirements?

Promises stressed:

- local working state != confirmed authority;
- device/route possession != Access;
- accessibility/degraded parity;
- assistance != authorship transfer;
- disclose less rather than more when safety is uncertain.

## SVT-14 — Conflicting legitimate actors and authority boundaries

**Families:** conflicting intent, authority, affected-party.

Context:

- Judge wants to amend judgment;
- Organizer believes recorded data should be capture-corrected or invalidated;
- support/admin can technically intervene;
- publishing authority may separately need to act on downstream release.

Challenge:

> Can conflicting actors each exercise only their legitimate semantic action without another actor's technical/process capability becoming substitute authority?

Promises stressed:

- Judge amendment != capture correction;
- Provenance explains rather than decides;
- technical privilege != semantic authority;
- Award/declaration/publication owners remain separate.

# 8. Risk-weighting rationale for Phase 016

- difficult or impossible reversal;
- privacy/disclosure asymmetry;

- multi-Concept coordination;
