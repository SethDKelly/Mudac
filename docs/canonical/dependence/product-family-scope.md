# Purpose

Own current **product-family scope selection** after Phase 012 whole-graph validation, counterexample audit and final consolidation.

This owner answers which dependence-valid/coherent subsets are actually adopted as current MUDAC product variants.

It does not redefine direct dependence, synchronization or Phase-013 mapping.

# Governing distinction

```text
dependence-valid
  != meaningful capability subset
  != adopted product variant
```

Likewise:

```text
Concept has no active instance in one Competition
  != Concept is omitted from the product variant
```

# Current adopted product family

MUDAC adopts exactly one current product/application variant:

> **PF-01 — MUDAC Live Competition Judging & Official Outcome**

PF-01 is the current full live student-competition judging/outcome product. It supports preparation, blinded judging, operational coordination, authoritative/correctable evidence, official outcome authority, optional recognition, continuity, and controlled external representation/release.

No reduced Concept subset is currently adopted as a separate edition, tier, deployment, or application.

# PF-01 capability envelope

PF-01 keeps all eighteen current Concepts in product scope:


This is product scope, not direct-dependence semantics.

It does not mean Competition directly depends on every Concept or that every Competition must instantiate/use every capability.

# Baseline product posture

PF-01 preserves:

- Competition + Team as the live competitor context;
- Division as current competitive/ranking context;
- Alias + Division as the current blinded Judge-facing Team representation baseline;
- Identity + Participation + Access for attributable scoped authority and disclosure control;
- Panel as reusable Judge grouping capability;
- Evaluation Occurrence + Evaluation Obligation + Rubric + Scorecard as distinct judging capabilities;
- Versioning + Provenance for authoritative Rubric/Scorecard lineage and correction;
- Outcome Declaration for ordinary official closeout;
- Award as supported but event-optional recognition;
- Export + Publication as supported controlled externalization/release capabilities.

# In-scope profiles inside PF-01

These are **not separate product variants**.

## Award-optional Competition

A Competition may define/confer no Award. Official Outcome Declaration does not require Award.

## Official but non-public

A current Outcome Declaration may exist without Publication.

## Prepared representation without release

An Export may exist without Publication.

## Public non-official representation

Export + Publication may release legitimate non-official material when source/disclosure/publishing authority permit it.

## Public official result

Public official-result release uses:

```text
Outcome Declaration + Export + Publication
```

plus applicable current source/disclosure/publishing authority.

## Exceptional/no-result official disposition

Outcome Declaration may record a legitimate exceptional/no-result disposition with an accepted reconstructible basis; normal evidence/rank must not be fabricated simply to force an ordinary outcome.

## Paper/electronic/mixed continuity

These remain capture/channel profiles over the same semantic product variant.

# Coherent contractions not currently adopted

## Truly no-Division product

**Coherent, not adopted.** Current blinded disclosure and Rank semantics are Division-sensitive. Future adoption requires explicit policy/composition generalization; do not manufacture placeholder Division state.

## No-Panel product

**Coherent, not adopted as a separate variant.** PF-01 retains reusable evaluator grouping. Operational reassignment does not remove Panel capability from product scope.

## Occurrence-only product

**Coherent, not adopted.**

## Obligation-only product

**Coherent, not adopted.**

## Working Scorecard-only product

**Coherent only without authoritative claims; not adopted.**

## Completed judging-only product without Outcome Declaration

**Coherent at dependence level, not adopted.** Current project purpose includes official outcome formation.

## Recognition-only final product

**Coherent, not adopted.** Recognition does not replace official result authority.

## Intentionally non-blinded competitive judging

**Out of current scope.** Current purpose/policy requires bias-aware protected Team identity during ordinary judging. Future adoption requires explicit purpose/policy reassessment.

## Authoritative evaluation without Versioning/Provenance

**Invalid under current authority semantics.**

## No-Division rank-derived recognition

**Deferred / not adopted.** Current Rank is Division-scoped; future adoption requires Rank/Ranking Readiness/Award generalization.

# Product-versus-state rule

Do not create product variants merely from:

- Award present/absent;
- Export present/absent;
- Publication pending/completed/withdrawn;
- paper versus electronic capture;
- Draft versus authoritative Scorecard;
- Event Completed versus Finalized;
- current versus Affected/Superseded authority;
- ordinary versus successor Outcome Declaration;
- discretionary versus rank-derived Award selection.

These are configuration, lifecycle, policy, authority, or channel profiles inside PF-01.

# Composition revalidation result

**No immediate Phase-011 reopening is required for PF-01.**

Phase 011 already models the full eighteen-Concept application family selected here and preserves the necessary distinctions among identity/participation/access, Panel/occurrence/responsibility, Draft/authority, calculation/recognition/officiality, and source/Export/Publication/delivery.

# Final Phase-012 audit

PF-01 survives counterexample audit and final canonical reconciliation unchanged.

```text
new direct edge required: NO
PF-01 scope change required: NO
Phase-010 reopen required: NO
Phase-011 reopen required: NO
Phase-012 repair required: NO
```

Representative challenges resolved without scope change include:

- Competition with no Award;
- official-but-non-public operation;
- Export without Publication;
- pre-closeout Competition state;
- ad-hoc Judge substitution while Panel capability remains supported;
- single-cohort operation while PF-01 retains current Division-sensitive ranking/disclosure semantics;
- Access included for protected actions despite having no universal outgoing actor dependency;
- authoritative evaluation requiring Versioning/Provenance by capability rather than universal direct edges;
- exceptional/no-result Outcome Declaration without fixed Team/evaluation-source direct dependencies;
- paper capture without universal Export dependence.

Phase 013 mapping, Phase 014 familiarity/genericity refinement, and Phase 015 whole-system integrity analysis are complete. PF-01 remains the sole adopted variant entering Phase 016 scenario/misfit validation.

# Future variant revalidation routes

If current unadopted contractions are promoted later, revalidate the natural owner instead of changing dependence to mimic incumbent composition.

## No-Division

Revalidate anonymity/disclosure policy, occurrence presented-context composition, Division-scoped Rank, Ranking Readiness, and rank-derived Award/outcome composition.

## No-Panel

Revalidate evaluator-set establishment, occurrence preparation/begin, obligation establishment, and live-operations assumptions.

## Occurrence/Obligation contractions

Revalidate evaluation occurrence/obligation composition, Scorecard authority composition, and outcome evidence-eligibility assumptions as appropriate.

## Completed judging-only

Revisit project purpose/mandate as well as Competition closeout and Outcome Declaration composition; this is not merely a synchronization change.

## Intentionally non-blinded judging

Revisit P-02/P-08 and anonymity/disclosure policy before composition.

## No-Division ranked recognition

Revalidate Rank scope, Ranking Readiness, Award composition, and later mapping of ranking context.
