## Current product-family scope

MUDAC currently adopts one primary product/application variant:

> **PF-01 — MUDAC Live Competition Judging & Official Outcome**

PF-01 keeps all eighteen current Concepts in the supported capability envelope because the current mandate spans preparation, blinded live judging, coordination, authoritative/correctable evidence, official outcome formation, optional recognition, continuity and controlled representation/release.

This is product scope rather than direct-dependence semantics.

## In-scope profiles inside PF-01

Current product scope supports, without creating separate product variants:

- Competition with no Award;
- official outcome without Award;
- official-but-non-public state;
- Export prepared without Publication;
- controlled public non-official material;
- public official-result release;
- exceptional/no-result official disposition with an accepted reconstructible basis;
- paper/electronic/mixed continuity.

Concept inactivity in one Competition does not imply Concept omission from PF-01.

## Unadopted/deferred contractions

Current coherent contractions not adopted as separate products include truly no-Division, no-Panel, occurrence-only, obligation-only, working-Scorecard-only, completed judging-only, and no-Division ranked recognition variants.

Intentionally non-blinded competitive judging is out of current scope under the current fairness/disclosure baseline.

Authoritative evaluation without Versioning/Provenance is invalid under current authority semantics.
