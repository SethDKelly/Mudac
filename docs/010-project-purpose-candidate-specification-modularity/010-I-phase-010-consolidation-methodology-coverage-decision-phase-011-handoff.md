## Judging Encounter split

The previous Judging Encounter mixed:

- bounded historical occurrence truth; and
- evaluator responsibility/completion state.

Those purposes can change independently. The current model therefore owns them separately as:

- **Evaluation Occurrence** — what bounded evaluation event happened, under what presented context, with which actual participants and validity/history;
- **Evaluation Obligation** — who is responsible for producing qualifying independent judgment and whether that responsibility is Outstanding, Satisfied, Excused, Cancelled or succeeded/reassigned.

A completed occurrence no longer waits for every obligation to resolve.

## Competition reduction and Outcome Declaration promotion

Competition remains the lifecycle/context owner for one competition occurrence, including Finalized as a lifecycle state.

The identity, immutable basis, Affected state and predecessor/successor history of official declared outcome content now belong to **Outcome Declaration**.

Calculated result, declared official authority and external Publication remain distinct.

## Rejected overloaded merges remain rejected

Phase 010 explicitly rejected:

- Actor Context as a Concept merge of Identity + Participation + Access;
- Evaluation Work Item as a merge of occurrence + responsibility + independently authored judgment;
- Authority History as a merge of Versioning + Provenance;
- External Representation & Release as a merge of Export + Publication;
- monolithic Outcome as a combination of sufficiency/calculation/order/recognition/reconciliation/declaration.

## Rejected/derived subjects remain outside the Concept catalog

- Coverage / Evaluation Sufficiency remains derived factual sufficiency;
- governed exception disposition remains separate from factual sufficiency;
- Reconciliation remains an Organizer process/work mode;
- Aggregate, Rank and Readiness remain derived mechanisms;
- Recovery/Continuity remains a cross-cutting purpose obligation;
- Correction remains owner-specific behavior/composition rather than one umbrella Concept.

# 7. Completeness corrections closed before exit
