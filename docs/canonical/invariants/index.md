# Cross-Cutting Invariants

Stable normative constraints that span multiple Concepts, policies, mechanisms, or experience contracts.

* [Judge Independence](judge-independence.md) - Judge evaluation remains independent from peer results and standings.
* [One Logical Scorecard](one-logical-scorecard.md) - One Judge Participation × Encounter yields at most one weighted evaluation.
* [Missing Is Never Zero](missing-never-zero.md) - Absence of evaluation is not a numeric judgment.
* [Organizer Does Not Become Judge Author](organizer-not-judge-author.md) - Process/capture authority cannot silently become evaluation authorship.
* [Current vs Historical Truth](current-vs-historical-truth.md) - Current corrected state does not overwrite what was historically observed.
* [Calculated Is Not Official](calculated-not-official.md) - Computability/readiness does not establish declared official outcome.
* [Official Is Not Automatically Public](official-not-automatically-public.md) - Finalization and publication remain separate authority boundaries.
* [Capture-Channel Parity](capture-channel-parity.md) - Paper/electronic paths preserve one evaluation model and weight.
* [Accessibility Semantic Parity](accessibility-semantic-parity.md) - Accessibility adaptations preserve domain meaning and authority.
* [Truthful Authority Under Uncertainty](truthful-authority-under-uncertainty.md) - Unknown persistence/authority cannot be presented as confirmed success.
