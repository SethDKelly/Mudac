# Invariant

Changing capture channel must not change the identity of Team/Judge/Encounter, exact Rubric Version, Criterion/Note semantics, Judge authorship, or evaluation weight.

Assistance or Organizer transcription may change capture actor, never semantic evaluator. Mixed paper/electronic traces converge on one logical Scorecard.

See [Continuity & Paper](../policies/continuity-paper.md).
